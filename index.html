What does an Accessibility & Front-End Development Manager do?

A good question for an abstract title. My work is better described as leading the Accessibility, Front-End Performance, and Design Systems Development teams. Often, this means I am creating processes, gathering metrics, and coaching people to improve quality at every stage of our web and app experiences.
